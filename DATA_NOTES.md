# Data notes

## Map-selected Sentinel-2 data (v0.4)

- Catalogue: Element 84 Earth Search, collection `sentinel-2-c1-l2a` (Collection 1, public L2A surface reflectance and SCL COGs).
- No provider images are fetched until the user chooses a region/dates and requests a pair. The navigation basemap is separate from the selected Sentinel-2 observations.
- Bands: B04/B03/B02, with the collection's per-band scale and offset applied once. Both dates use the same fixed 0–0.3 reflectance-to-uint8 mapping, not separate histogram stretches.
- Cloud quality: SCL native resolution 20 m; only classes 4/5/6 are usable. Classes 3/8/9/10 receive an additional buffer (60 m by default). Snow, defective, dark/shadow, unclassified and nodata pixels are excluded. Classification errors and haze remain possible.
- Geometry: WGS84 polygon, local UTM CRS, one snapped 10 m or 20 m grid. Outside-polygon pixels and invalid pixels on either date are excluded from detection.
- Bundles record requested and actual dates, scene IDs, raw STAC items, quality policy/statistics, source scaling, grid transform/CRS and the selected polygon. RGB and quality GeoTIFFs retain spatial metadata. The decoded PNG pair and usable mask support offline replay.
- Attribution: Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data; use the acquisition years in the exported scene metadata. Review the provider's licence before redistributing.

Successful retrieval and a clear SCL mask do not establish change-detection accuracy. Retain independent annotations and record the same tuning/evaluation details listed below.
